# Asimov 1 locomotion policy checkpoint — 2026-10-26 lower-body 27-DOF, torso IMU

- Velocity-commanded locomotion policy for Asimov 1, trained in Isaac Lab with PPO and adversarial motion priors (AMP).
- Trained on the 27-DOF Asimov 1 model (6DOF wrists and a 2-DOF neck). The policy controls the 12 leg joints without observing the 15 waist, arm, wrist and neck joints, which follow randomized upper-body motion during training.
- The IMU is read on the torso (`waist_yaw_link`), above the waist yaw joint, instead of on the pelvis.
- Exported from `model_9999.pt` in the `2026-10-26_18-59-36_lower_body_27dof_4gpu_4096` run.
- `policy.onnx` is the exported policy for inference, producing leg joint-position actions.
- `policy.pt` is the same policy as TorchScript, for running it from PyTorch.
- `agent.yaml` records the actor-critic and AMP training settings, including the motion data configuration.
- `env.yaml` records the simulation and locomotion task settings, including observations, actions, commands, and rewards.
- `code_state.yaml` records the code the policy was trained with: the commit, the robot model, the package versions, a hash of the network's code, the joints and gains the policy's inputs and outputs resolved to, and the sha256 of the uploaded `env.yaml` and `agent.yaml`.
- Training and evaluation code: [menloresearch/cyclotron](https://github.com/menloresearch/cyclotron).

## Torso-mounted IMU

Base angular velocity and projected gravity in the actor observation are
measured at `waist_yaw_link`, so they rotate with the waist yaw joint. A
runtime must feed this policy readings from an IMU mounted on the torso (or
transform pelvis readings into the torso frame through the measured waist yaw
angle). Feeding pelvis-frame IMU readings unchanged will mismatch training
whenever the waist is turned.

## Independent upper-body control

The actor receives joint-position and joint-velocity feedback only for the 12
leg joints and outputs actions for those same joints. A separate controller
supplies position targets for the 15 upper-body joints: waist yaw, both arms
(shoulder pitch, roll, yaw, elbow, wrist yaw), the 6DOF wrists and the neck
(yaw, pitch). Those targets are not generated by this ONNX policy. The actor
does not receive upper-body joint observations or targets; it still observes
torso angular velocity and projected gravity, which reflect the robot's
response to upper-body motion.

During training the upper body follows a coordinated motion sampler
(independent, mirrored, preset-pose and neutral motions, waist yaw within
`[-0.5, 0.5]` rad). The grippers are locked at their zero pose and are not
controlled. Upper-body joint feedback remains available to the critic during
training; the exported actor does not require it.

The previous-action observation contains the 12 raw leg actions without
zero-padding, for a total observation size of 45.

## Ankle settings

- Ankle-roll action scale is `0.08`; the other leg joints use `0.25`.
- Ankle-roll position targets are clipped to `[-0.09, 0.09]` radians after applying the action scale and default pose.
- Ankle-pitch stiffness/damping are `224.42` / `10.201`.
- Ankle-roll stiffness/damping are `35.2` / `1.6`.

Use `env.yaml` for the full actuator settings, default pose, and action configuration.

## How to run

Load `policy.onnx` with ONNX Runtime, or `policy.pt` with PyTorch, in an Asimov 1
runtime that supports 12 leg actions, independent upper-body position targets,
a torso-frame IMU, and the 45-value actor observation recipe in `env.yaml`.

The runtime builds observations and runs the policy at 50 Hz. It converts each
raw leg action into a position target using
`default_joint_pos + action_scale * action`, then applies the ankle-roll target
clipping. These operations happen outside the ONNX graph. Deployment metadata
in the ONNX records the action joint order, scales, offsets, clips, and PD gains.

## Inputs and outputs

- **Input:** float32 `obs`, shape `[1, 45]`: torso angular velocity (3), torso
  projected gravity (3), velocity command `vx, vy, wz` (3), leg joint positions (12),
  leg joint velocities (12), and the previous raw leg actions (12).
  Joint positions and velocities follow the motor-slot order in `env.yaml`;
  upper-body joint observations are omitted.
- **Output:** float32 `actions`, shape `[1, 12]`, in this order:
  left hip pitch, hip roll, hip yaw, knee, ankle pitch, ankle roll;
  right hip pitch, hip roll, hip yaw, knee, ankle pitch, ankle roll.
- **Control rate:** 50 Hz (`sim.dt = 0.005`, `decimation = 4`).
- Trained in Isaac Lab (PhysX), 4096 environments on 4 GPUs.

The full observation order and action joint list are recorded in `env.yaml`.
